LASD vegetarian and vegan menu

Source: https://www.lasdschools.org/menus. Menu data last updated 2026-09-15.

🧇 breakfast, 🍴 lunch, 🍕 vegetarian, 🥗 vegan. 🅴 or 🅼 marks an item that is only on the elementary or only on the junior high (Blach, Egan) menu; no mark means it is on both. Allergens follow the colon. Every meal comes with fruit, vegetables, and 1% or non-fat milk. Menus are subject to change.

Subscribe to the calendar

Calendar link: https://swamisun.github.io/lasd-menu/menu.ics (open in Apple Calendar · download · markdown)

  • Google Calendar: Other calendars, +, From URL, paste the link. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, usually every 12 to 24 hours.
  • Apple Calendar: File, New Calendar Subscription, paste the link (or use the "open in Apple Calendar" link above).

Reminders

Every event carries two alarms: 9 PM the night before and 7 AM the day of. Whether you see them depends on the calendar app:

  • Apple Calendar strips alarms from subscriptions by default. Right-click the calendar, Get Info, and uncheck "Remove: Alerts".
  • Google Calendar ignores alarms inside subscribed feeds. Instead, open the calendar's settings, find "All-day event notifications", and add two: "the day before at 9:00 PM" and "the same day at 7:00 AM". Same result.
  • Outlook uses the embedded alarms as is.
