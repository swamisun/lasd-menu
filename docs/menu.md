# LASD vegetarian and vegan menu

Source: [https://www.lasdschools.org/menus](https://www.lasdschools.org/menus). Menu data last updated 2026-09-15.

Legend: 🧇 breakfast, 🍴 lunch, 🍕 vegetarian, 🥗 vegan. 🅴 or 🅼 marks an item that is only on the elementary or only on the junior high (Blach, Egan) menu; no mark means it is on both. Allergens follow the colon. Every meal comes with fruit, vegetables, and 1% or non-fat milk. Menus are subject to change.

Calendar subscription: `https://swamisun.github.io/lasd-menu/menu.ics`
